Indian Engineering Student Placement Analysis

This dashboard analyzes placement outcomes across engineering disciplines, identifying salary trends, specialization impact, and employability drivers. The objective was to uncover patterns influencing placement success and compensation outcomes.

Problem

Engineering graduates face uneven placement outcomes depending on branch, academic performance, and soft skill indicators. Stakeholders need clarity on what factors most strongly correlate with successful placement.

Approach

Exploratory data analysis and feature segmentation were used to evaluate salary distributions, branch-level performance, and placement probability. Comparative filtering enables dynamic analysis by academic and demographic factors.

Key Insights

• Clear salary clustering across specific branches • Strong relationship between academic performance and placement rate • Noticeable compensation gap between top and median performers
